# Customer Acquisition

## Funnel
Eligible audience → reached → sample offered/distributed → QR scan or WhatsApp start → first paid order → second paid order.

## Procedures
1. Assign distinct campaign identifiers/QR codes to placement, shift/time, sampling session, and offer version where feasible.
2. Log sample count separately from scans and paid orders.
3. Record timestamps so the three-hour offer window can be measured.
4. Track both list value and realized revenue after the 50% first-order offer / 25% same-day offer.
5. Measure incremental first orders, not just orders that would have happened anyway. Use a simple comparison across time windows or offer cohorts where feasible.
6. Keep WhatsApp ordering friction low: clear menu, clear pickup location, order format, estimated ready time, and confirmation.
7. Do not promise a ready time unless operationally supportable.
8. Track opt-in/consent and honor opt-outs; do not spam employees or scrape private contact data.
9. Calculate acquisition metrics only from observed counts: scan rate, scan-to-order conversion, sample-to-paid conversion, cost per acquired customer, and 7/14/30-day repeat rate.

## Outputs
- Campaign design and message variants
- QR tracking plan
- Sampling shift plan
- WhatsApp order flow
- Offer economics
- Weekly funnel dashboard
