# Growth Analytics

## Minimum event ledger
Date/time | campaign/source | QR or referral code | new/repeat customer (pseudonymous ID) | order ID | items/basket | list value | discount | realized revenue | sample flag | fulfillment time | reward cost/status.

Do not store unnecessary personal data. Use customer consent and limited access.

## Core metrics
- Paid orders/day and by shift
- Realized average order value
- Net sales and contribution under the agreed 50% cost assumption
- Sample-to-scan and scan-to-paid conversion
- First-to-second-order conversion within 7/14/30 days
- Repeat-order share
- Referral share of new paid customers
- Offer redemption and discount dependency
- Order-ready time and pickup wait time
- Complaints, refunds, cancellations, and food waste/sampling volume

## Experiment standard
Every test must state: hypothesis, audience, intervention, duration, primary metric, guardrail metric, minimum usable sample or practical stopping rule, result, decision. Change one major variable at a time when possible.

## Reporting
- Daily operating snapshot
- Weekly funnel and cohort review
- Monthly forecast-vs-actual reconciliation
- Record formula definitions and source of each number.

## Quality controls
- Do not infer causation from correlation.
- Do not present tiny samples as reliable benchmarks.
- Show counts alongside percentages.
- Report missing data and attribution limitations.
