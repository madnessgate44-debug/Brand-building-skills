# Market Intelligence

## Purpose
Build a reliable local market picture that informs positioning, pricing, offers, and channel choices.

## Research procedure
1. Define the exact catchment: site, walk-time radius, nearby entrances, workplaces, shifts, and realistic pickup path. If the exact mall name is unknown, research Smouha/Alexandria broadly and mark the local gap explicitly.
2. Map competitors into:
   - Direct Asian / Chinese food
   - Fast-casual substitutes (burgers, fried chicken, pizza, sandwiches)
   - Delivery apps and nearby pickup options
   - Convenience-store and snack substitutes
3. For each competitor, collect: source URL, access date, menu date if available, item name, displayed price, portion/size if stated, offer, order channel, pickup/delivery, service promise, location, and confidence.
4. Prioritize official menus and live ordering platforms; use directories and social posts only as secondary evidence. Do not combine stale and current prices without labels.
5. Compare like with like. A soup, a main, and a combo are different baskets. Compare bundle contents and portion sizes where possible.
6. Identify whitespace: speed, reliable ready-to-pickup, value, meal variety, late/early shift coverage, and frictionless WhatsApp ordering.
7. Cite each external factual claim and state when current price verification was not possible.

## Outputs
- Competitor matrix
- Price-band summary by category
- Direct vs indirect competitor map
- Customer alternatives and unmet needs
- Evidence gaps and field-research checklist
- Implications for ASIA's menu, offers, and positioning

## Quality gates
- No invented prices or businesses.
- No false precision about market size.
- Separate a user's estimate of 3,000 employees from an independently verified count.
- Include observation date and source quality for price comparisons.
