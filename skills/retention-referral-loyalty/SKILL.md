# Retention, Referral & Loyalty

## Goals
Increase repeat paid orders and trusted word-of-mouth without giving away more value than the incremental contribution can support.

## Referral system
- Each customer receives a unique referral code or QR.
- Reward only after the referred customer completes a valid paid order.
- Prevent self-referrals, duplicate accounts/orders, cancelled orders, and repeated reward claims.
- Track inviter, referred customer, order ID, reward status, and redemption.
- Do not invent points-per-order or free-meal threshold; calculate options after menu/offer economics and the user's intended reward are confirmed.

## Loyalty system
- Define a simple earn rule, redemption threshold, expiry policy, exclusions, and clear customer-facing terms.
- Model reward liability and redemption cost using the 50% total-cost planning assumption, with a sensitivity check for heavily discounted orders.
- Measure enrollment, active members, repeat rate, redemption rate, incremental frequency, and contribution after rewards.
- Avoid rewarding purchases that would have happened anyway without measuring a baseline.

## Retention cadence
- Thank-you / order feedback after first order
- Useful reminder timed to likely next purchase, with opt-out
- Reorder shortcut via WhatsApp
- Referral prompt after a positive experience
- Weekly cohort review of first-to-second-order conversion

## Outputs
- Referral and loyalty rules
- Reward economics
- Customer communication sequence
- Abuse controls
- Cohort and redemption scorecard
